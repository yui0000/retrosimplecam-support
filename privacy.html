Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Information We Collect

Retro Simple cam does not collect any personal information. The app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no data is transmitted to any external server.

Camera and Photo Library

Retro Simple cam uses your device's camera to show a live preview with its film-style filter and to take photos. It requests photo library access only to save the photos you take. Location information is not recorded in your photos.

Photos Stored in the App

To let you readjust the filter strength later, the app keeps your most recent 100 photos in its own storage on your device. Older photos are deleted automatically. These photos are excluded from iCloud backup, are never uploaded or shared with third parties, and are removed when you delete the app.

Third-Party Services

Retro Simple cam uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies to any purchase transactions.
